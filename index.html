About Me

Turning Ad Spend Into
Profitable Growth

I'm Sabir Lalahiane, a performance marketing strategist based in Marrakech, Morocco. Over the past five years, I've helped businesses across Europe, the Middle East, and North Africa transform their digital advertising from a cost center into a revenue engine.

My approach is simple: every dollar spent must be accountable. I combine deep platform expertise with rigorous data analysis to build campaigns that don't just generate clicks—they generate customers. From high-ticket e-commerce to complex B2B funnels, I architect advertising systems designed for sustainable scale.

With a background in IT development, I bring a technical edge to marketing that most strategists lack. I don't just run ads—I build integrated tracking infrastructures, optimize conversion funnels, and ensure every touchpoint is measured and improved.
